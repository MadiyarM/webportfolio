System Deficit

Real estate transactions are plagued by information asymmetry. Traditional agents often prioritize commission speed over buyer fit, leading to suboptimal asset allocation and buyer frustration.

  • [!] High commission friction: 3-6% lost per transaction.
  • [!] Latency in market updates: 24h+ delay in new listings.
  • [!] Biased valuation models favoring sellers.

Logic Solution

HonestREBot introduces a neutral algorithmic broker. By leveraging direct API access and real-time scrapers, it provides an unfiltered view of the market with zero human intervention.

  • [OK] Direct-to-API data fetching: 0.5s refresh rate.
  • [OK] Automated heuristic valuation (ML-driven).
  • [OK] Zero-fee matchmaking infrastructure.
